## The Pitch
Do you like listening to music? - Most likely.  
Do you wanna make music?? - Surely.  
Want some free and open source software for your music desires?
Then you need InstroSoft! My fully custom music creation software designed for InstroPad(1) but perfect for anyone!

## The Features
- Drag and drop file capabilities - drag any sound file onto the pads and play it to your hearts content!
- Volume slider - hmm I wonder what that does...
- Play with your keyboard - you can trigger the pads using your numpad or top row number keys!
- Designed for InstroPad(1) - works seamlessly with InstroPad

## How to use me
1. Drag your sound files onto the pads to assign them
2. Grab a keyboard
3. Use said keyboard to trigger the the keys or use your mouse - why?
4. Make some fire beats
